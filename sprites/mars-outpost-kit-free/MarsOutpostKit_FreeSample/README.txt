MARS OUTPOST KIT - FREE SAMPLE
Made by GameShipKit (https://gameshipkit.com)

This sample has 2 player ships, 2 enemy ships, a blue laser, a small
explosion, 4 pickups, 6 props, the sand ground tiles, the rock plateau
autotile, a star layer and the Mars planet. Everything is animated where
it should be and comes with Godot SpriteFrames and the Unity importer.

The full kit adds 16 player ship sheets, the mothership and 3 more
enemies, 8 more effects, 8 more pickups with animations, 12 more props,
the ice autotile, all parallax star layers and the Mars outpost map:
https://gameshipkit.com/mars-pack/

Sizes, folders and engine notes are the same as in the full kit:
player ships are 32x40 frames facing up, tiles are 16x16, sheets have
2px padding and come with Aseprite JSON.
